What it is

A free reading tracker and reader that works inside your browser. It opens PDF, EPUB, audio, text, Markdown and web page files you choose, and text you paste. It doesn't upload, host, share or publish your files.

Read aloud, text mode, text recognition and logging can make mistakes

Text pulled out of a PDF can be out of order or incomplete, text recognized from scanned pages can contain errors, header and footer cleanup can occasionally hide a real line, voices can mispronounce words, and automatic page counts are estimates. Check the original when accuracy matters.
