# Release notes · Reading (reading.mcbflow.com)

Newest first. One entry per release, in plain words a user would understand. The full technical log is in CHANGELOG.md, and the same notes show in the app under Settings & Help › Release notes.

## 1.4 · October 15, 2026
- Optional free mcbflow.com account: sign in from Settings or the Data menu → Sync across devices to keep your books, progress, notes, quotes, Daily Review and reading history the same on your phone and computer. Without an account, everything works as before and nothing leaves your device.
- Your PDF, EPUB and audio files stay on the device you added them on. On another device, attach the file to the same book to read there. Places in files, bookmarks, reader settings and theme stay on each device.
- Erase all data warns that it also deletes your synced data when you're signed in.

Internal build 3.9.0.

## 1.3 · October 14, 2026
- Moved to reading.mcbflow.com. Data saved at mbreading.online doesn't carry over: export a backup there and import it here.
- Storage names simplified.

Internal build 3.8.0.

## 1.2 · 2026-10-14
- On computers, the search box, backup status and logo spacing now look exactly like the other mb apps: same size, same "Search…" text and the same "/" shortcut.
- Tidier top bar that matches the other mb apps. On phones the app name no longer gets cut off, and search, Undo, Data and the light/dark switch are matching square buttons.
- The light/dark switch shows a sun and a moon so it's clear what it does.
- On computers, Data, Add book and Log reading keep their text labels at every window size, and Data shows a small dot when a backup is due.
- Your books, notes and history are untouched.
